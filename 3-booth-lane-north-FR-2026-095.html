Proposed Scope of Works

Below is the full scope of what we'll be supplying, installing, and commissioning at 3 Booth Lane North. Everything below is taken directly from the HVAC design pack approved under the planning application (drawings 01–05, Odour Risk Assessment OD657218 and the DAA Group noise impact assessment). No changes to the approved design are proposed. The system is grouped into five areas: kitchen extract, ductwork and distribution, odour control, kitchen fresh air supply, and commissioning and handover.

Kitchen Extract System

  • Wall-mounted stainless steel extract canopy above the cook line Manufactured in 304-grade stainless steel with an external dull polish grain and internal stainless filter housing. Fitted with removable, washable stainless steel baffle grease filters and an integrated LED lighting system (electrical connection to the lights by others).
  • Helios GBW 560/4 kitchen extract fan Installed on anti-vibration rubber mounts with flexible connectors on inlet and outlet to stop noise and vibration transferring into the ductwork and structure. Fan interlocked to the odour control equipment so it cannot run independently of the filtration.
  • R02-5-1500 attenuator — 1,500 mm long Fitted after the fan to achieve the −10 dB insertion loss required by the DAA Group noise impact assessment. Acoustic media lined with Melinex so the extract system can be steam cleaned as part of routine TR19 maintenance.
  • High-velocity discharge nozzle terminating 1 m above ridge 500 mm diameter discharge exiting through the rear of the roof and terminating via a high-velocity nozzle 1 m above ridge level, positioned as shown on the roof plan and elevations, to meet the dispersion requirements of the odour risk assessment.

Ductwork & Distribution

  • 700 × 350 mm kitchen extract ductwork with cleaning access Galvanised extract ductwork run from the canopy with cleaning access hatches every 2 m, giving full access for TR19 grease cleaning along the entire run.
  • 500 mm diameter extract ductwork from filtration to discharge Ductwork rising from the ground floor to the first floor odour control unit, then on to the extract fan, attenuator and roof discharge, fully supported and sealed throughout.
  • 315 mm diameter fresh air supply ductwork Circular supply ductwork from the fresh air fan into the kitchen, terminating in air diffuser grilles positioned as shown on the drawings to return 85% of the extracted air back into the kitchen.

Odour Control

  • UVO1000 UV-ozone unit installed within the canopy Fitted ahead of the filtration system to break down odorous compounds in the extracted fumes before they reach the ductwork and filters.
  • 2 × G4 disposable pre-filters Highly efficient at capturing oil and grease particles ahead of the electrostatic unit. Replacement recommended every 2–3 weeks depending on usage.
  • ESP 5000B electrostatic precipitator Collects the fine smoke, grease and oil particles filtered out of the exhaust, delivering the high level of odour control required by Odour Risk Assessment OD657218 (risk score 26 — High) and the EMAQ/DEFRA guidance.

Supply Kitchen Fresh Air System

  • Gebhardt DDMP 10/10 filtered fresh air supply fan Pre-filtered motorised supply unit installed on anti-vibration rubber mounts and isolators with flexible connectors, bringing filtered fresh air into the kitchen to replace what the extract removes.
  • CP03-CAP-0355-2D attenuator Installed after the supply fan to achieve the insertion loss set out in the DAA Group noise impact assessment.
  • External weather louvre grille and supply diffusers Fresh air intake via an external weather louvre, delivered into the kitchen through supply diffuser grilles at the positions shown on the design drawings.

Commissioning & Handover

  • Full system commissioning and airflow balancing Once installation is complete, we run all systems up, verify supply and extract performance against design, and balance airflows so every area is properly ventilated and pressure-balanced.
  • BESA-compliant installation documentation Installation carried out under our BESA certification (HV020676) to DW 144 / DW 172 standards. Commissioning certificates issued on handover.
  • Product data sheets and client walkthrough on handover Technical data sheets for all installed equipment provided on the day. We walk you through the systems — how to operate them, how to change filters, what to check day to day, and the recommended service schedule.
Exclusions

Building works (including builders' work openings through walls, floors and the roof, structural supports, weathering and making good) are excluded. All electrical works are excluded — power supplies, isolators, control wiring, the fan-to-filtration interlock wiring and connections to the canopy lighting are to be carried out by others to the relevant standard. Scaffolding and access equipment for the roof and external works are excluded and to be provided by others. Gas interlock, fire suppression, planning application fees and ongoing filter replacements are not included.
