What's Included

Proposed Scope of Works

Below is everything covered under this quote at 167 Drury Lane. The callout charge is included in the total — there is nothing to add on top.

Silencer & Flexible Connections

  • Supply and install high-efficiency silencer In-line acoustic attenuator selected to suit the existing extract fan and ductwork, fitted within the extract run to reduce fan noise breakout to the surrounding area.
  • 2 × flexible connections Flexible duct connections supplied and fitted at the fan inlet and outlet, isolating the fan from the rigid ductwork so vibration is not carried along the duct run.

Anti-Vibration Mounting

  • 6 × anti-vibration rubber mounts Rubber anti-vibration mounts supplied and fitted to the fan and silencer supports, breaking the mechanical path between the running plant and the structure.
  • Replace existing roof bracket with anti-vibration bracket Existing rigid roof bracket removed and replaced with an anti-vibration bracket. The fan is isolated, lifted, reinstated on the new mounting and reconnected, with all fixings and sealing included.

Callout & Ancillaries

  • All fixings, brackets and fitting kit Every support, fixing and sealing material needed for a secure, properly supported installation is included in the price — no hidden extras.
  • Callout charge — included The attendance and callout fee is included within the total quoted price. No separate callout invoice will be raised.

Commissioning & Handover

  • System run-up and operational check Once fitted, we run the fan up, check airflow is unaffected, and confirm the reduction in noise and vibration at the fan and roof mounting. We don't leave until it's right.
  • Handover and warranty documentation issued on the day All relevant documentation issued on completion. Your warranty period begins from the date of practical completion.
