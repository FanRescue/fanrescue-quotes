Proposed Scope of Works

Below is the full scope of what we'll be supplying, installing, and commissioning at JoyPark, Westfield White City, as detailed on Dee Solutions drawings 1454/M/200 P2 (Proposed AC Layout), 1454/M/100 P2 (Proposed Ventilation Layout) and the 1454-ES-01 Rev D AC Schedule. The works are grouped into five areas — removal of the existing air conditioning unit, the water-cooled VRV air conditioning system, the condenser water connection, the new ventilation branches and grilles, and cleaning and re-balancing of the retained ductwork — followed by commissioning and handover.

Removal of Existing Air Conditioning Unit

  • Removal of 1 × existing ceiling-mounted air conditioning unit (approx. 1600 × 1600 × 900mm) One heavy existing unit located in the ceiling at approximately 2.4m high. Our F-Gas registered engineers professionally recover the refrigerant charge and issue a recovery certificate, then cut the unit into smaller sections as necessary and bring it down to floor level. Removal of only this one unit is included; no other existing plant is included in the strip-out.

Air Conditioning — Water-Cooled VRV Heat Recovery System

  • Daikin RWEYQ26-T9Y1B water-cooled VRV IV W+ heat recovery condensing system (CON-1) Supplied as two floor-mounted modules — 1 × RWEYQ14-T9Y1B (CON-1A) and 1 × RWEYQ12-T9Y1B (CON-1B) — positioned in the back-of-house plant location shown on 1454/M/200, complete with outdoor multi-connection kit, anti-vibration mounting and interconnecting refrigerant pipework between modules. Heat recovery configuration allows simultaneous heating and cooling across the tenancy.
  • 2 × Daikin BS6Q14AV1B six-port branch selector boxes (BS-01, BS-02) Ceiling-mounted in the kitchen area as drawn, complete with refnet joint and all associated three-pipe refrigerant connections, supports and insulation. These allow each indoor unit to select heating or cooling independently.
  • 9 × Daikin FXFQ-B round-flow ceiling cassettes 1 × FXFQ50B (FCU-01 Party Room), 4 × FXFQ63B (FCU-02 Waiting Area, FCU-03 Play Area, FCU-07 Reception, FCU-11 Snacks) and 4 × FXFQ100B (FCU-04, FCU-05, FCU-06, FCU-10 Play Area), each fitted with a Daikin BYCQ140EB black decoration panel to suit the dark play-area environment as scheduled on 1454-ES-01. Installed with condensate drainage via integral drain pumps, suspended from the structure and set out to the reflected ceiling plan.
  • 2 × Daikin FXAQ25A wall-mounted units FCU-08 Office and FCU-09 Kitchen, wall-mounted as drawn, complete with condensate drainage and refrigerant connections.
  • Three-pipe refrigerant pipework distribution Full liquid, suction and high/low-pressure gas pipework from the condensing modules via the branch selector boxes to every indoor unit, run in the ceiling void along the routes shown on 1454/M/200. Phosphoric-acid deoxidised copper to Daikin specification, Armaflex insulated throughout, with all refnet joints, supports, sleeving and fire-stopping at penetrations included.
  • Pressure test, evacuation, vacuum drying and refrigerant charge Nitrogen pressure test to EN 378-2, deep vacuum, and additional R410A charge calculated from the installed liquid-line lengths and BS unit factors. Carried out by our F-Gas registered engineers (FGAS2001890) with the fluorinated greenhouse gas label completed and fixed to the unit.
  • 11 × Daikin BRC1KPD51W Madoka Plus wall controllers One local controller per indoor unit, including the front-of-house controller arrangement for the Waiting and Play areas set to run from return-air sensors with FCU DIP switches configured for high-level installation as noted on 1454/M/200. All F1/F2 transmission wiring between condensing modules, BS boxes, indoor units and controllers included.
  • Daikin DCM601B51 Intelligent Touch Manager with DMS502A51 BACnet gateway Central controller installed in the tech room / plant area as drawn, giving scheduled control and monitoring of all eleven indoor units, with the BACnet interface supplied and configured ready for connection to the landlord or tenant BMS.
  • Condenser water interlock and flow switch connection Flow switch and pump-run interlock terminated at each RWEYQ module (X2M interlock terminals) so the units cannot run without proven water flow, as required by Daikin. The condenser water pipework itself is covered under Condenser Water Connection below.

Condenser Water Connection

  • Isolate and drain the existing condenser water pipework The existing system is isolated at the landlord tenant connection, drained down and disconnected from the controls before any dismantling begins.
  • Dismantle the existing hydraulic system The whole existing condenser water hydraulic system — pipework, pump, valves, strainers and ancillaries — is dismantled and cleared from its current position.
  • Reinstall to the new condensing unit positions Pipework, pump, isolation valves, strainers, commissioning sets, flow switch and gauges reinstalled to the new RWEYQ module positions shown on 1454/M/200, with components replaced as needed where the existing items are not fit for reuse, and connected to the units.
  • Pressure test, flush and water treatment Completed circuit pressure tested, then flushed and dosed with water treatment in line with the landlord's guidance for the Westfield condenser water system.
  • Fill, commission and connect to controls System filled, vented and commissioned to the design flow rate, with the pump and flow switch connected to the controls and interlocked with the VRV condensing modules.

Ventilation — New Extract Branches & Grilles

  • New 450Ø extract connection onto the existing landlord ductwork New galvanised 450Ø spiral extract duct connected into the retained system as shown on 1454/M/100, fabricated and installed to BESA DW/144 with all supports, fittings and sealing.
  • New 200Ø extract branch to the Waiting Area 200Ø galvanised branch terminating in a new 450 × 450 ceiling grille (EG-2) with volume control damper, serving the waiting area.
  • New 200Ø extract off the existing spigot to the Party Room 200Ø branch from the existing spigot terminating in a new 450 × 450 ceiling grille (EG-3) with volume control damper.
  • New 200Ø fresh air branch with cross-talk attenuator 200Ø fresh air connection to the Party Room complete with in-line cross-talk attenuator to limit noise transfer between spaces, as drawn.
  • New 150Ø extract disc valves — Snacks Counter and Vending 150Ø galvanised branches with volume control dampers terminating in ceiling disc valves over the snacks counter (EG-4) and the snack/drink vending machines (EG-1).
  • New 125Ø extract branch to the Office 125Ø branch with volume control damper and 125Ø disc valve serving the office (EG-5), plus the 150Ø disc valve extract to the kitchen / WC area as drawn.
  • 300 × 300 door transfer grille Transfer grille supplied and fitted in the door to the snack/vending room for fresh air make-up, as noted on 1454/M/100. Door undercuts for air transfer are builder's work — see Exclusions.
  • All brackets, supports, flexible connections and fixings Complete fitting kit for the new ductwork, sealed and supported to DW/144, with volume control dampers on every branch so the system can be balanced.

Existing Ductwork — Clean & Re-Balance

  • Cleaning of the retained landlord extract ductwork The existing 450Ø extract ductwork shown as retained on 1454/M/100 is internally cleaned before the new branches are connected, so the new grilles are not fed from a dirty system. Access panels fitted where required for cleaning access.
  • Re-balancing of the combined system Once the new branches are on, the whole extract system is re-balanced using the volume control dampers so each grille and disc valve delivers its design airflow, with results recorded on the commissioning sheet.

Commissioning & Handover

  • Full system commissioning and airflow balancing Once installation is complete, we run all systems up, verify supply and extract performance against design, and balance airflows so every area is properly ventilated and pressure-balanced.
  • BESA-compliant installation documentation Installation carried out under our BESA certification (HV020676) to DW 144 / DW 172 standards. Commissioning certificates issued on handover.
  • Product data sheets and client walkthrough on handover Technical data sheets for all installed equipment provided on the day. We walk you through the systems — how to operate them, how to change filters, what to check day to day, and the recommended service schedule.
Exclusions

Any structural platform, plinth or support steelwork required for the air conditioning condensing modules is excluded and to be provided by others. Also excluded: painting of the retained ductwork black (noted on 1454/M/100) — we do not carry out decoration works; any works to the landlord's central condenser water plant, cooling towers or risers upstream of the tenant connection; electrical supplies, distribution board ways, isolators and containment to the plant positions (by others) — we carry out the final power connections to the condensing modules, BS boxes and indoor units from the isolators provided; BMS-side programming and integration beyond supply and configuration of the BACnet gateway; door undercuts, builder's work, structural openings, making good and ceiling reinstatement; Westfield / landlord approvals, permits and out-of-hours access charges; asbestos survey or removal; and ongoing TR19 cleaning of the kitchen extract system.
