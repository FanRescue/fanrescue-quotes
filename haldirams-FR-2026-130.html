What's Included

Proposed Scope of Works

Below is everything covered under this service at 19-20 Irving Street. All indoor and outdoor units on the system are included.

Pre-Service Checks

  • System survey and asset record Every indoor and outdoor unit identified and logged — make, model, serial number, location and refrigerant type — so you have a complete asset register for the system.
  • Pre-service operating check Each system run up before any work starts and any existing faults, error codes or user complaints noted, so the report shows the condition we found as well as the condition we left.
  • Area protection Dust sheets and drip trays laid beneath indoor units before covers are removed, to protect floors, furniture and food preparation areas.

Indoor Units — All Units

  • Filters removed, cleaned and refitted Return-air filters on every indoor unit removed, washed, dried and refitted. Damaged filters noted for replacement.
  • Evaporator coil cleaning Coils cleaned and treated with a non-acidic coil cleaner to restore heat transfer and airflow and reduce odours.
  • Fan wheel and casing clean Indoor fan wheels, louvres and internal casings cleaned of dust and debris to reduce noise and improve air delivery.
  • Condensate tray, drain and pump Condensate trays cleaned and treated, drains flushed and checked for free flow, and condensate pumps tested where fitted — the most common cause of leaks into the ceiling.
  • Electrical and sensor checks Visible terminals and connections checked, and room and pipe sensors checked for correct readings.
  • Controls and settings Wall controllers and remotes checked for correct operation, set points, modes and timer settings, and casings and grilles wiped down.

Outdoor Units — All Units

  • Condenser coil cleaning Outdoor coils cleaned with a coil cleaner and rinsed to remove dirt, grease and debris so the units reject heat properly — particularly important on a site near kitchen extract discharge.
  • Fan, guard and motor Condenser fan blades, guards and motors checked for damage, security and abnormal noise.
  • Electrical inspection Terminals checked and tightened, contactors and visible wiring inspected, and local isolators checked for condition.
  • Mountings and supports Wall brackets, frames and anti-vibration mounts inspected for corrosion and security.
  • Pipework, insulation and service valves Visible refrigerant pipework, insulation and service valve caps inspected for damage, deterioration or signs of oil staining.
  • Surroundings and airflow Debris cleared from around the units and any obstruction to airflow noted.

Performance Checks & Reporting

  • Operating checks on every system Each system run in cooling and heating; operating pressures, temperatures, running currents and supply/return air temperatures recorded against expected values.
  • F-Gas leak check and records Leak check carried out in line with the F-Gas Regulation and the equipment record updated where the system requires it.
  • Post-service run test All systems run and checked after the service so we leave every unit operating correctly.
  • Written service report Service report issued with the asset register, readings taken, before-and-after condition, and any defects or recommendations clearly flagged and prioritised.
Not Included

Refrigerant top-up or recovery, replacement parts (pumps, fan motors, PCBs, sensors and the like), and any repair or remedial works found during the service are not included. Anything we find will be reported and priced in writing for your approval before any work is carried out.
