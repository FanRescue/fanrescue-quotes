What's Included

Proposed Scope of Works

Below is everything covered under this quote at 90 Leather Lane. Engineer attendance is included within the quoted prices — no separate callout charge.

AC & AHU Service — £350 + VAT

  • Full service of the air conditioning system Operational and safety checks, filter and coil cleaning, condensate drain check, operating pressure and temperature checks, and a full controls test. Carried out by our F-Gas registered engineers.
  • Full service of the air handling unit (AHU) Inspection of fans, motors, belts, and bearings; internal clean of the unit; damper and controls check; and airflow verification to confirm the unit is performing as it should.
  • Engineer attendance — included Attendance is included within the quoted prices. No separate callout invoice will be raised.

AHU Filter Supply & Install — £450 + VAT

  • Supply of replacement AHU filters New filters of the correct grade and sizes for the installed unit, supplied and delivered to site.
  • Removal, disposal, and installation Old filters removed and disposed of responsibly. New filters fitted and correctly seated, with the unit resealed and checked before handover.

Reporting & Handover

  • System run-up and operational check Once the service and filter works are complete, we run both systems up, confirm performance, and check all controls are operating correctly. We don't leave until everything is working.
  • Service report and warranty documentation A full service report is issued on completion. Your warranty period begins from the date of practical completion.
