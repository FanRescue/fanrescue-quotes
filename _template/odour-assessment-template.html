What's Included

Scope of the Assessment

The Odour Risk Assessment report covers the full scope below. Everything is included in the fixed fee — there are no additional charges for revisions to the report arising from information provided at the outset.

Odour Risk Evaluation

  • Assessment of the proposed kitchen extraction system design We review the proposed extraction arrangement — canopy type, fan specification, ductwork route, and discharge point — against current EMAQ+ guidance to determine the baseline odour risk profile for the site.
  • Identification of sensitive receptors We assess the surrounding land use — residential properties, neighbouring businesses, and any other sensitive receptors in the vicinity of {{SITE_REFERENCE}} — and evaluate the likely odour impact on each.
  • Discharge point and dispersion assessment We evaluate the proposed discharge location, height, and direction relative to sensitive receptors, identifying any risk of odour recirculation or inadequate dispersion given the {{RECEPTOR_CONTEXT}} context.

Odour Control Recommendations

  • Odour control measures specification Where risk is identified, we provide clear, specific recommendations on appropriate odour control measures — whether that is a carbon filtration system, UV-C treatment, electrostatic precipitator, or a combination — sized to the kitchen type and menu profile.
  • Grease filtration and pre-treatment recommendations Effective odour control begins with proper grease management. We assess the grease filtration arrangement and recommend appropriate pre-treatment where required, ensuring the odour control plant is not compromised by grease carry-over.
  • Maintenance and operational guidance Odour control systems require regular servicing to remain effective. The report includes guidance on maintenance intervals and operational requirements to keep the system performing to the standard assessed.

Report & Documentation

  • Formal written Odour Risk Assessment report A professionally produced written report suitable for submission to the Local Planning Authority, Environmental Health, or inclusion in a planning application. The report references current EMAQ+ guidance throughout and sets out findings, risk levels, and recommendations clearly.
  • PDF delivery within agreed turnaround The completed report is delivered as a PDF to the email address provided. Turnaround is typically {{TURNAROUND_DAYS}} working days from receipt of the proposed system design information.
