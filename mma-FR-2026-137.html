Introduction

Hi,

Thank you for asking Fan Rescue to quote for the mechanical installation at Wardour Food Hall, 181-185 Wardour Street. Set out below is our quote for the ventilation and air conditioning across both the basement and ground floor levels.

In the basement, the package covers four stainless steel kitchen canopies with grease-tight extract ductwork, three-stage odour control (disposable pre-filter, double-pass electrostatic precipitator and double-pass carbon filtration), an inverter-controlled extract fan with silencers and a high-velocity discharge so the system can terminate at low level, a filtered kitchen fresh air supply interlocked with the extract, toilet extraction, Daikin air handling units for general ventilation and Daikin air conditioning. At ground floor level it covers the extract and fresh air ductwork with high-level odour control (double-pass ESP and two UVO 1000 UV-ozone units), Daikin ventilation and Daikin air conditioning. We will also prepare the design for landlord approval before works start. Everything is supplied, installed and commissioned by our own in-house engineers, with handover documentation on completion. The price is broken down by floor and by system in the investment section so you can see exactly where the cost sits.

Proposed Scope of Works

Below is the full scope of what we'll be supplying, installing and commissioning at Wardour Food Hall, 181-185 Wardour Street. The works are grouped into pre-construction and project management, then each system by floor — basement first, then ground floor — followed by controls and electrical connections, and commissioning & handover.

Pre-Construction & Project Management

  • Pre-start site survey and setting out Detailed survey of both floors before fabrication to confirm duct routes, plant and equipment positions, ceiling voids, penetrations and the discharge location, and to set out hanger and equipment positions on site.
  • Design coordination and landlord submission Preparation of layout drawings, equipment selections and technical data sheets for landlord approval, and coordination of our services with the shopfitter, electrician and other trades so ductwork, pipework and plant fit first time. (Design fee shown separately below.)
  • RAMS, inductions and permits Site-specific risk assessments and method statements issued before we start, attendance at site inductions, and compliance with the landlord’s and main contractor’s permit-to-work and hot-works procedures.
  • Dedicated project manager and programme A single point of contact at Fan Rescue from order to handover, an installation programme agreed with you and your shopfitter, and regular progress updates through the job.
  • Protection, housekeeping and waste Protection of finishes in our work areas, daily clean-up, and removal of our own packaging, offcuts and installation waste from site.

Basement — Kitchen Extract System

  • Four stainless steel kitchen canopies Supply and installation of four kitchen canopies over the cooking line in grade 304 stainless steel, sealed grease-tight to DW 172, each with extract plenum, stainless steel baffle grease filters, grease collection channel and sealed LED lighting. Canopy sizes are confirmed against the final appliance line-up before fabrication.
  • Canopy suspension, fixing and sealing Each canopy hung from the soffit on drop rods and support channel, levelled and sealed to the wall with food-safe sealant so there are no grease or dirt traps behind the canopy.
  • Extract header with balancing dampers Common extract header collecting all four canopies, with a balancing damper on each canopy spigot so each canopy can be set to its design share of the airflow at commissioning.
  • Grease-tight extract ductwork to DW 172 Galvanised extract ductwork with fully sealed grease-tight joints, falls arranged to avoid grease pooling, and all brackets, hangers, support steel and mounts.
  • Cleaning access hatches to TR19 Grease Grease-tight cleaning access hatches at bends, fittings, plant and straight runs at the spacings required by TR19 Grease, so the system can be cleaned and inspected from day one without cutting into the ductwork.
  • Extract fan with inverter speed control Kitchen extract fan selected for the system duty with inverter (variable speed) control, so the kitchen team can turn the extract up or down to suit cooking load and the duty is set accurately at commissioning.
  • Fan silencers and anti-vibration mounts Attenuators (silencers) either side of the extract fan, with the fan on anti-vibration mounts and flexible connections, to keep fan noise and vibration out of the kitchen and the building structure and away from neighbours.
  • High-velocity discharge for low-level termination Because the extract terminates at low level rather than above roof, the discharge is fitted with a high-velocity terminal sized to throw the plume clear of the building and away from nearby windows and openings, in line with EMAQ+ guidance for low-level discharge. Weatherproofed, with bird and vermin mesh, and the outlet velocity set and verified at commissioning.

Basement — Odour Control

  • Stage 1 — disposable pre-filter section Disposable pre-filter section ahead of the odour control plant to catch residual grease and particulates and protect the ESP and carbon stages downstream.
  • Stage 2 — double-pass electrostatic precipitator (ESP) Double-pass ESP to remove fine grease, smoke and cooking fume from the extract airstream, with removable cells that can be taken out and washed.
  • Stage 3 — double-pass carbon filtration Double-pass activated carbon filter section downstream of the ESP to remove cooking odours before discharge, with the carbon sized for the extract volume to give the contact time EMAQ+ recommends for the kitchen’s odour risk.
  • ESP power supply and interlock ESP power supply unit wired so the ESP only runs when the extract fan is running, with on/fault indication so the kitchen team can see the odour control is working.
  • Odour control plant installation and service access Odour control plant installed in sequence on the extract with sealed transitions, access doors on every stage, and clear service access left for cell washing, filter changes and carbon replacement.

Basement — Kitchen Fresh Air Supply

  • Fresh air intake Fresh air intake connection with weather louvre and bird mesh, positioned away from the extract discharge so the supply does not draw kitchen exhaust back in.
  • Supply fan with pre-filter section Kitchen supply fan with fresh air pre-filter section to keep dust and pollen out of the kitchen and the supply ductwork.
  • Fan silencers and anti-vibration mounts Attenuators (silencers) on the supply fan, with anti-vibration mounts and flexible connections to keep supply fan noise down.
  • Supply ductwork, grilles and diffusers Galvanised supply ductwork to DW 144 with volume control dampers, supports and brackets, and supply grilles and fresh air diffusers positioned to deliver air into the kitchen without disturbing the canopy capture.
  • Extract / supply interlock Supply fan interlocked with the extract so it can only run when the extract is running, balanced so the kitchen is held at a slight negative pressure and cooking odours stay in the kitchen rather than drifting into the front-of-house.

Basement — Toilet Extraction

  • Toilet extract fan Extract fan serving the basement toilets, sized to Building Regulations Part F (at least 6 l/s per WC) and wired for run-on so each room clears after use.
  • Toilet extract ductwork, valves and discharge Ductwork, ceiling extract valves and backdraught protection, discharging to outside, with all supports and fixings.

Basement — General Ventilation

  • Daikin air handling units Supply and installation of Daikin air handling units providing filtered supply and extract ventilation to the basement back-of-house, storage and staff areas, sized to Building Regulations Part F.
  • Ventilation ductwork, grilles and diffusers Galvanised supply and extract ductwork with volume control dampers, supports and brackets, and supply diffusers and extract grilles to each area.
  • Ventilation controls Daikin controls for the air handling units, set up at commissioning with run times and fan speeds to suit the trading pattern.

Basement — Air Conditioning

  • Daikin outdoor condensing units Daikin outdoor units installed in the agreed plant position on anti-vibration mounts, brackets or frames as the location requires.
  • Daikin indoor units Daikin indoor units to the basement areas, sized for the cooling and heating loads and positioned for even air distribution.
  • Refrigerant pipework Insulated copper refrigerant pipework brazed under nitrogen, fully supported and run neatly between indoor and outdoor units.
  • Condensate drainage Condensate drainage from each indoor unit to the nearest suitable drain point, with condensate pumps where a gravity fall is not possible.
  • Controls, testing and F-Gas Wired controller for each zone. Pressure testing, evacuation and refrigerant charging carried out by our F-Gas registered engineers (FGAS2001890), with F-Gas records issued at handover.

Ground Floor — Kitchen Extract & Odour Control

  • High-level odour control — double-pass ESP Double-pass electrostatic precipitator on the ground floor kitchen extract to remove fine grease, smoke and cooking fume, with removable cells that can be taken out and washed.
  • 2 no. UVO 1000 UV-ozone units Two UVO 1000 ultraviolet / ozone units installed in the extract after the ESP to break down remaining cooking odour and grease vapour in the ductwork before discharge.
  • UVO interlocks and safety switching UVO units interlocked with the extract fan so the lamps only run when air is moving, with safety switches on the access panels so the lamps switch off whenever a panel is opened.
  • Extract fan, silencers and anti-vibration mounts Extract fan with attenuators (silencers) either side, anti-vibration mounts and flexible connections to keep noise and vibration out of the building.
  • Grease-tight extract ductwork and access hatches Galvanised extract ductwork to DW 172 at ground floor level with sealed grease-tight joints, cleaning access hatches at TR19 Grease intervals, and all brackets, hangers, supports and mounts.

Ground Floor — Fresh Air Supply

  • Supply fan with pre-filter section Fresh air supply fan with pre-filter section, attenuators (silencers), anti-vibration mounts and flexible connections.
  • Supply ductwork, grilles and diffusers Galvanised supply ductwork to DW 144 with volume control dampers, supports and brackets, and supply grilles and fresh air diffusers, balanced at commissioning.

Ground Floor — General Ventilation

  • Daikin air handling units Supply and installation of Daikin air handling units providing filtered supply and extract ventilation to the ground floor areas, sized to Building Regulations Part F.
  • Ventilation ductwork, grilles and diffusers Galvanised supply and extract ductwork with volume control dampers, supports and brackets, and supply diffusers and extract grilles to each area.
  • Ventilation controls Daikin controls for the air handling units, set up at commissioning to suit the trading pattern.

Ground Floor — Air Conditioning

  • Daikin outdoor condensing units Daikin outdoor units installed in the agreed plant position on anti-vibration mounts, brackets or frames as the location requires.
  • Daikin indoor units Daikin indoor units to the ground floor, sized for the cooling and heating loads and positioned for even air distribution.
  • Refrigerant pipework and condensate Insulated copper refrigerant pipework brazed under nitrogen and fully supported, with condensate drainage from each indoor unit and condensate pumps where a gravity fall is not possible.
  • Controls, testing and F-Gas Wired controller for each zone. Pressure testing, evacuation and refrigerant charging by our F-Gas registered engineers, with F-Gas records issued at handover.

Controls & Electrical Connections

  • Kitchen ventilation controls Kitchen controls for the extract and supply systems on each floor — on/off, fan speed adjustment and running indication — positioned where the kitchen team can reach them.
  • System interlocks Supply fans interlocked with their extract, and the ESP and UVO odour control interlocked to run only with the extract fan, so the systems always run together as designed.
  • Final power and control wiring Final power connections from the isolators provided by others to every fan, ESP, UVO unit, air handling unit and air conditioning unit we install, plus all control cabling between our own equipment.

Commissioning & Handover

  • Airflow commissioning and balancing Every system run up, extract and supply volumes measured and balanced against design, canopy extract rates checked, and the kitchen pressure balance confirmed on both floors.
  • Odour control and discharge checks ESP and UVO units checked for correct operation and interlocking, carbon filters installed and sealed, and the high-velocity discharge velocity measured and recorded.
  • Air conditioning commissioning Each Daikin system run in cooling and heating, operating pressures and air temperatures checked, controls set up, and F-Gas records completed.
  • BESA-compliant installation documentation Installation carried out under our BESA certification (HV020676) to DW 144 / DW 172 standards. Commissioning certificates and F-Gas records issued on handover.
  • Handover pack and client walkthrough Product data sheets and operating and maintenance information for all installed equipment. We walk your team through the systems — how to run them, how to clean the grease filters and ESP cells, when carbon and pre-filters need changing, and the recommended service and TR19 cleaning schedule.
Exclusions

Gas interlock and gas pipework; automatic fire suppression (see below); fire-rated ductwork or fire-rated builder's enclosures where the kitchen extract passes through other compartments; builder's work, structural openings through walls, floors and roof, and making good; painting or cladding of ductwork; mains electrical supplies, DB ways, isolators and containment to plant positions — Fan Rescue carries out final power connections from the isolators provided; crane hire; planning, Building Control and discharge of conditions, and any acoustic or odour reports; asbestos survey or removal; out-of-hours working; parking, permits and congestion/ULEZ charges; any works not expressly listed in the scope above.
