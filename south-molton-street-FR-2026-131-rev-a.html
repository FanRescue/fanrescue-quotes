Proposed Scope of Works

Below is everything covered under this quote at 11 South Molton Street, set out in five parts with the price for each shown alongside. Parts A to D are fixed; for Part E, choose either Option 1 or Option 2.

Part A
Ground Floor Cookline Canopy & Extract Connection
£3,700 + VAT

Canopy

  • Cookline canopy — approx. 2 m New stainless steel extract canopy, approximately 2 m long, supplied and installed over the ground floor cookline, complete with grease baffle filters and grease collection.
  • Fixing and sealing Canopy suspended from the soffit on suitable fixings, set level and sealed, with clearances to the cooking equipment below agreed with your team before installation.

Ductwork & Connection

  • New extract ductwork Extract ductwork from the new canopy run to and connected into the existing extraction system, with access panels for future cleaning.
  • Connection to the existing system Tie-in to the existing ductwork made and sealed, with the joint and any disturbed sections checked for leakage.

Commissioning

  • Run-up and performance check System run up once connected, and extract checked at the new canopy and the existing canopies on the same system. We will confirm the existing fan is carrying the additional canopy and report in writing if any adjustment or upgrade is needed.
Part B
Basement Extract System — Full Service & Clean
£1,100 + VAT

Canopies & Ductwork

  • Canopies serviced and cleaned Grease filters removed, degreased and refitted; canopy internals, plenums and grease channels cleaned.
  • Extract ductwork cleaned Accessible extract ductwork cleaned through the existing access panels. Any section we cannot reach is recorded on the report so you know exactly what was and wasn’t cleaned.

Extract Fan

  • Fan cleaned and serviced Impeller, casing and motor housing degreased and cleaned; bearings, belts and drive checked, running current measured against the nameplate, and mountings and flexible connections checked.

Reporting

  • Post-clean report Report issued with before-and-after photographs, readings, and any defects or recommendations clearly flagged.
Part C
Basement Fresh Air Supply System — Supply & Install
£3,300 + VAT

Fresh Air Plant

  • High-pressure supply fan Motorised high-pressure supply fan supplied and installed, mounted on anti-vibration fixings with flexible connections to keep noise and vibration out of the structure.
  • Filter section Disposable filter fitted on the fan intake to keep the fan and ductwork clean.
  • Silencer Attenuator fitted after the fan to keep fan noise out of the basement.

Distribution

  • Ductwork Supply ductwork run from the fan to the basement areas served, supported and sealed throughout.
  • Diffusers Supply diffusers fitted to distribute fresh air evenly across the basement.
  • Commissioning System run up and airflow checked at each diffuser, and the system balanced before handover.
Part D
Cold Room & Freezer Room Refrigeration
£7,500 + VAT

Strip-Out

  • Existing plant removed Existing condensing unit and control panel isolated, refrigerant recovered under F-Gas regulations, and the plant removed and disposed of.

New Refrigeration Systems

  • Two new refrigeration systems One system for the cold room and one for the freezer room, each with an indoor evaporator and outdoor condensing unit, supplied and installed with refrigerant pipework, insulation and condensate drainage.
  • Freezer room floor Proper insulated flooring supplied and installed to seal the room for freezer use.
  • Digital controls Digital temperature controllers supplied and installed for both rooms, with defrost control on the freezer.

Commissioning

  • Testing and pull-down Systems pressure tested, evacuated and charged, then pulled down to temperature and checked. Refrigerant records completed for your F-Gas log.
Before We Start

Both rooms need to be emptied of stock for the changeover. We’ll agree the date with you in advance so you can plan around it.

Part E
Ground Floor Air Conditioning — Choose One Option
from £6,300 + VAT

Option 1 — Two New Ducted Units · £7,860 + VAT

  • Two ducted indoor units Two new ducted indoor units supplied and installed on the ground floor.
  • One outdoor unit One new outdoor unit supplied and installed to serve both indoor units.
  • Pipework, drainage and controls All refrigerant pipework, condensate drainage, controller connections and interconnecting cabling included, with final power connections made from isolators provided.
  • Commissioning System pressure tested, evacuated, charged and commissioned, with controllers set up and demonstrated to your team.

Option 2 — One New Ducted Unit + Relocation · £6,300 + VAT

  • One new ducted unit One brand new ducted air conditioning unit supplied and installed, with refrigerant pipework, condensate drainage, controller connection and interconnecting cabling.
  • Existing wall-mounted unit relocated Existing wall-mounted unit pumped down, disconnected and relocated to the other corner, with pipework and drainage re-run as needed, then re-tested and recommissioned.
Not Included

Electrical supplies, isolators and distribution board ways to the new canopy, fans, refrigeration and AC plant (by your electrician — we make the final connections from the isolators provided); builder’s work, structural openings, core drilling and making good; any new external louvre or opening for the fresh air intake; fire dampers or fire stopping where ductwork crosses a fire compartment line; fire suppression to the new canopy (can be priced separately on request); cold room or freezer panels, doors, door heaters and strip curtains beyond the flooring described in Part D; gas works and gas interlock alterations; planning or building control applications and fees; decoration. Anything we find during the works will be reported and priced in writing for your approval before any additional work is carried out.
