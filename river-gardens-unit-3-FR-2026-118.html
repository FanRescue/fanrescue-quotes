Proposed Scope of Works

Below is the full scope of what we'll be supplying, installing, and commissioning at Unit 3, Block 5, The River Gardens. The package covers six areas: the VRV air conditioning system, heat recovery ventilation, toilet extraction, fire dampers and volume control, ductwork and distribution, and commissioning and handover.

VRV Air Conditioning System

  • Daikin REYQ8U VRV heat recovery condensing unit (8HP, three-phase) Supplied, positioned and installed in the designated plant position. Heat recovery operation means the system can heat and cool different units at the same time, moving rejected heat between zones rather than wasting it — the most efficient VRV configuration Daikin make.
  • Daikin BS12Q14AV1B 12-port branch selector box Installed at high level with its own condensate drainage connection. The BS box is what gives each cassette independent heating or cooling — every tenant space runs its own climate regardless of what the neighbours are doing.
  • 10no Daikin FXFQ roundflow ceiling cassettes (8no FXFQ20B + 2no FXFQ25B) One cassette serving each commercial unit in line with the Turnkey Planning & Design layout, each fitted with a BYCQ140E decoration panel for a clean, flush ceiling finish with 360° air distribution.
  • 10no Daikin Madoka wall-mounted controllers Individual temperature, mode and fan-speed control in each unit — simple, modern touch controllers mounted at accessible height.
  • 1no dedicated Daikin split system (RXM35A9 / FFA35A9) A standalone 600×600 fully flat cassette with decoration panel and Madoka controller, installed complete with its own condensing unit, pipework and condensate.
  • Refrigerant pipework — installed, insulated and pressure tested All interconnecting copper pipework sized to the Daikin piping schedule, insulated throughout, nitrogen pressure tested, deep vacuumed and charged with the required additional refrigerant. All refrigerant works carried out under our F-Gas certification (FGAS2001890).
  • Condensate drainage Condensate runs from all indoor units and the branch selector box, installed at self-draining gradients to suitable discharge points.
  • Controls, transmission wiring and final connections F1F2 transmission and P1P2 controller wiring throughout the system. We carry out the final power connections to the condensing unit, BS box and indoor units from the isolators provided.

Ventilation System — Heat Recovery Fresh Air

  • 2no Daikin VAM800J8 heat recovery ventilation units Ceiling-void mounted air handling units delivering tempered fresh air and balanced extract through cross-flow heat exchangers — recovering heat from the outgoing air so the fresh air supply doesn't cost you comfort or energy. Each unit complete with its own Madoka controller.
  • Fresh air intake and exhaust ductwork to atmosphere Intake and discharge runs connected to the designated louvre and riser positions, keeping intake and exhaust properly separated.
  • Supply and extract distribution across the units Ducted distribution at high level with ceiling supply diffusers and extract grilles serving the commercial units and circulation areas per the proposed layout.
  • Filtration Panel filters fitted to both airstreams on each VAM unit, accessible for routine cleaning and replacement.

Toilet Extraction System

  • Dedicated WC mechanical extract A separate mechanical extract system serving the unisex WCs, accessible WC and tea point — kept independent of the fresh air system so odours are extracted at source and discharged, never recirculated.
  • Extract valves and high-level ductwork Extract valves in each WC, ducted at high level to the discharge position.
  • Fan controls with run-on Extract fan controls configured with run-on operation so the WCs continue to be purged after use.

Fire Dampers & Volume Control

  • Fire dampers at compartment penetrations Supplied and installed wherever the ventilation and extract ductwork passes through fire-rated walls and compartment lines identified on the drawings, maintaining the fire integrity of the building.
  • Access panels to all fire dampers Access panels fitted at every damper position for inspection, drop-testing and future maintenance compliance.
  • Volume control dampers throughout VCDs installed on branches across the supply and extract distribution, giving us full control at commissioning to balance airflows accurately to every area.

Ductwork & Distribution

  • Galvanised sheet metal ductwork to DW/144 All new distribution ductwork manufactured and installed to BESA DW/144, properly sealed and supported.
  • Complete ducting fitting kit Bends, reducers, plenums, flexible connections, drop rods, supports, fixings and sealants — everything required to install the distribution as drawn, supplied as part of the package.
  • Grilles, diffusers and louvres Supply diffusers, extract grilles and terminal fittings across the system, selected for airflow performance and a tidy finished appearance.
  • Thermal insulation to ductwork where required Fresh air and supply runs insulated to prevent condensation and heat loss through ceiling voids.

Commissioning & Handover

  • Full system commissioning and airflow balancing Once installation is complete, we run all systems up, verify supply and extract performance against design, and balance airflows so every area is properly ventilated and pressure-balanced.
  • BESA-compliant installation documentation Installation carried out under our BESA certification (HV020676) to DW 144 / DW 172 standards. Commissioning certificates issued on handover.
  • Product data sheets and client walkthrough on handover Technical data sheets for all installed equipment provided on the day. We walk you through the systems — how to operate them, how to change filters, what to check day to day, and the recommended service schedule.

Also excluded: builders work, structural openings and making good; ceiling grids, bulkheads and reinstatement; fire stopping of builders-work openings; drainage beyond the condensate terminations described; BMS integration; landlord, planning or building control approvals; and out-of-hours working unless agreed in advance.
