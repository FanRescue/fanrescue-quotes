Introduction

Hi Danny,

Thanks for having us out to 21 Monmouth Street. Proposed Scope of Works

Below is the full scope of what we'll be supplying, installing, and commissioning at 21 Monmouth Street. The works are grouped into four areas: air conditioning and refrigeration, refrigeration pipework, ventilation fans and extract cleaning, and commissioning and handover.

Air Conditioning & Refrigeration

  • Service of existing air conditioning, cold room and standing refrigeration units Full service and inspection of the existing air conditioning units, cold room refrigeration and standing refrigeration units. Filters cleaned, coils and condensers checked and cleaned, refrigerant charge and operating pressures verified, drainage cleared, controls and safety devices tested. Any faults found are reported with a recommendation before further work is carried out. F-Gas records updated where applicable.
  • Supply and install 1no. wall-mounted indoor unit and 1no. outdoor condensing unit Removal and responsible disposal of the existing faulty units, including recovery of refrigerant under our F-Gas registration. Supply and installation of a replacement wall-mounted indoor unit and matched outdoor condensing unit, connected to existing services where they are suitable for re-use, then pressure tested, evacuated, charged and commissioned.

Refrigeration Pipework

  • Refrigeration pipework amended and re-routed Existing refrigeration pipework relocated away from the extract ductwork so the duct run can be reached for cleaning and ongoing maintenance. New runs insulated, clipped and supported correctly, with condensate drainage adjusted to suit the revised route.
  • Pressure test, evacuation and recommissioning All altered circuits pressure tested and leak checked, evacuated to remove moisture and non-condensables, recharged to manufacturer specification and recommissioned. Operating pressures and temperatures recorded, and F-Gas documentation updated on completion.

Ventilation Fans & Extract Cleaning

  • Fresh air supply fan — replacement The damaged fresh air supply fan removed and replaced on a like-for-like basis, reconnected to the existing supply ductwork and electrical supply, then run up and checked. Please note we require mains power to be live and available at the fan so we can test it and confirm its condition before removal — if power isn't available on the day the visit may need to be rescheduled.
  • Kitchen extraction system clean to TR19 Grease standard Deep clean of the kitchen extraction system — canopy, filter housings, accessible ductwork runs and fan housing — carried out to the BESA TR19 Grease standard. Access panels fitted where needed to reach enclosed sections. Before and after photographic report and a certificate of cleanliness issued on completion for your insurers.
  • Existing extract fan — assessment and replacement (provisional) The existing extract fan is assessed once the ductwork has been cleaned and we can see its true condition. If it can be cleaned, serviced and recommissioned rather than replaced, we'll confirm that in writing and adjust the final account accordingly. If replacement is required, the replacement fan and installation are carried within this quote.

Commissioning & Handover

  • Full system commissioning and airflow balancing Once installation is complete, we run all systems up, verify supply and extract performance against design, and balance airflows so every area is properly ventilated and pressure-balanced.
  • BESA-compliant installation documentation Installation carried out under our BESA certification (HV020676) to DW 144 / DW 172 standards. Commissioning certificates issued on handover.
  • Product data sheets and client walkthrough on handover Technical data sheets for all installed equipment provided on the day. We walk you through the systems — how to operate them, how to change filters, what to check day to day, and the recommended service schedule.
Exclusions

Excludes: electrical supply, isolation and any containment or power alterations required for the replacement fans (mains power must be live and available at the fresh air fan for us to test it); builder's work, making good and decoration; access equipment beyond standard steps and mobile tower; asbestos survey or removal; grease filter supply or replacement (quoted separately at booking if required); structural or roof works; out-of-hours or overnight working unless agreed in writing; and any remedial works identified once the ductwork is opened up and cleaned. Any work outside this scope will be priced and confirmed in writing before it goes ahead.
