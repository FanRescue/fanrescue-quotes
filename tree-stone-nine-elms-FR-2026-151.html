Proposed Scope of Works

Below is the full scope of what we'll be designing, supplying, installing and commissioning at 55 Nine Elms Lane, as per the architectural drawings and MEP information provided. The works are grouped into six areas: M&E design, cooling and heating, general ventilation, the kitchen extract system with fire-rated ductwork, the kitchen and dining fresh air system, and commissioning and handover.

M&E Design

  • Mechanical layout drawings for all systems Plan layouts for the cooling and heating, general ventilation, kitchen extract and fresh air systems, coordinated with the architectural drawings.
  • Landlord submission and approval We prepare the submission information, respond to the landlord's technical comments and update the drawings through to approval. £4,500 + VAT, included in the total price — payable up front and credited in full against the installation deposit.

Air Conditioning — Cooling & Heating

  • Daikin indoor units to the restaurant Daikin heat pump indoor units (or similar) to the dining and back-of-house areas as per the approved design, positioned to suit the ceiling layout, with return air and supply connections.
  • Daikin outdoor plant Daikin outdoor condensing units (or similar) at the landlord's agreed plant position, mounted on anti-vibration supports.
  • Refrigerant pipework Insulated copper refrigerant pipework with branch kits, brackets and fixings. Brazed under nitrogen, pressure tested, evacuated and gas charged by our F-Gas registered engineers.
  • Condensate drainage Condensate pipework from each indoor unit.
  • Controls Wired controllers for each zone, so front and back of house can be run independently.
  • Final power connections Final connections to the outdoor plant and indoor units from isolators provided by others.

General Ventilation

  • 2 × Daikin air handling units with battery heaters Two Daikin air handling units (or similar) with filters and battery heaters for tempered supply air, mounted on anti-vibration supports with flexible connections, as per the approved design.
  • Supply and extract ductwork and grilles Galvanised supply and extract ductwork with brackets and fixings, and grilles or diffusers with plenums and volume control dampers, coordinated with the ceiling layout.
  • Intake and exhaust connections Ductwork connections from the AHUs to the external intake and exhaust points (openings by others).
  • Toilet extraction Dedicated extract fan serving the WC areas, with ductwork, extract grilles and volume control dampers.
  • Back-area prep kitchen ventilation Supply and extract ventilation to the rear prep kitchen, with ductwork and grilles.
  • Fire dampers where required Fire dampers on the general ventilation system at compartment lines, as identified in the approved design and fire strategy, each with an access panel for inspection and testing.
  • AHU controls Controllers for the AHUs and battery heaters, with time scheduling and speed adjustment.

Kitchen Extract System

  • Kitchen extract fan Kitchen extract fan selected to the approved design, mounted on anti-vibration supports with flexible connections to stop noise and vibration transferring into the building.
  • Fan speed control Speed controller so the kitchen team can run the extract to suit service levels.
  • Extract acoustic attenuation In-line attenuator on the extract run, with acoustic media suitable for kitchen extract.
  • Extract ductwork, fitting kits, brackets and fixings All kitchen extract ductwork, bends, transitions, fitting kits, brackets and fixings from the cook line through to the riser connection.
  • Dining room extraction above tables Extraction above the dining tables, linked into the kitchen extraction system, with ductwork and diffusers to each table position.
  • TR19 cleaning access panels Grease-tight access panels along the extract run so the system can be cleaned and inspected to TR19 Grease.

Ductwork & Distribution

  • 2-hour fire-rated kitchen extract ductwork Fire-rated kitchen extract ductwork within the kitchen area and through to the riser, achieving a 2-hour fire rating.
  • Riser connection Fire-rated connection into the riser at the landlord's agreed connection point.

Supply Kitchen Fresh Air System

  • Fresh air supply fan with pre-filter Supply fan with pre-filter, on anti-vibration supports with flexible connections.
  • Battery heater Battery heater with controls to temper the incoming air, so the kitchen and dining area aren't hit with cold air in winter.
  • Supply-side acoustic attenuation In-line attenuators on the supply run to keep fan noise out of the dining area.
  • Kitchen supply ductwork and diffusers Galvanised supply ductwork and diffusers to the kitchen, balanced against the extract to keep the kitchen at slight negative pressure.
  • Dining area supply ductwork and diffusers Galvanised supply ductwork and diffusers with plenums and volume control dampers across the full dining area, coordinated with the ceiling layout.
  • Fresh air intake connection Ductwork connection to the external fresh air intake point (opening by others).

Commissioning & Handover

  • Full system commissioning and airflow balancing Once installation is complete, we run all systems up, verify supply and extract performance against design, and balance airflows so every area is properly ventilated and pressure-balanced.
  • Air conditioning commissioning and F-Gas records Refrigerant system run-up and performance checks in heating and cooling, with F-Gas records for the charged systems.
  • Fire damper testing Drop test of each fire damper on completion, with results recorded.
  • BESA-compliant installation documentation Installation carried out under our BESA certification (HV020676) to DW 144 / DW 172 standards. Commissioning certificates issued on handover.
  • Product data sheets and client walkthrough on handover Technical data sheets for all installed equipment provided on the day. We walk you through the systems — how to operate them, how to change filters, what to check day to day, and the recommended service schedule.
Exclusions

Builder's work, structural openings and making good; suspended ceilings, ceiling access panels and any painting or decoration of ductwork; fire stopping around service penetrations; electrical supplies, distribution board ways, isolators and containment to plant positions; gas works; fire alarm interfacing beyond a volt-free contact; landlord riser and roof works beyond our connection point; landlord, planning and building control fees; scaffolding or access equipment; out-of-hours working; asbestos survey or removal; wet chemical fire suppression and cold rooms (can be quoted separately); and any works not explicitly listed above unless separately agreed in writing.
